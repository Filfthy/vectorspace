AGENT X / VECTOR SPACE
BEYOND THE STORY: FACTION MISSIONS, REWARDS AND WEAPONS (DRAFT FOR REVIEW)
=========================================================================

Status: built in v400 (sections 1-4). Random encounters (section 4, last bullet)
are not built yet. Salvage, loot and selling were dropped by design.


DIRECTION
---------
* Agent X flies a drone. This is not a trading or cargo game.
* Items are earned through missions, or bought in the existing market. No
  loot tables, salvage economy or selling.
* The real gap is what there is to do after the VOX story, and what faction
  standing is for.
* Reuse the environments that already exist. Most new missions should be
  authored variations on proven environments, not new engines.


WHERE WE ARE NOW
----------------
* The VOX story is 12 missions. It can be finished at about level 7-9.
* Every faction contact already appears in it once or twice, but only as a
  favour VOX needs:
    Kudo Shang (OSD)       Emergency Defence, Source Trace, Factory Moon
    Corvella (Traders)     Missing Manifest
    Cassian (Jackals)      A Small Favour
    Navira (Helix)         Field Samples, Nest Strike
    Nyxo (QuickBite)       Hot Delivery, Route Interference
* After the story there are only the 9 board templates and VOX re-skins of
  them. Four templates stop scaling at risk 4-6.
* Standing does little: market grade +1/+2, a slightly higher risk ceiling,
  and three faction frames. Nothing past 60. QuickBite standing gives nothing.
* The style guide already plans one max-standing reward mission per faction,
  where the contact offers a lead and X must fly to earn the item. Not built.
* Built environments that are only reachable in DEV mode: dune armour field,
  swamp delivery, swamp console, forest wreckage investigation, space recovery
  pickup.


1. FACTION AGENT MISSIONS
-------------------------
Each of the five networks gets a short personal chain of three missions from
its named contact. The chains are separate from the VOX story and need no
story progress, only standing and level.

  STEP   UNLOCKS AT                  WHAT IT IS
  1      Favoured (25), level 6+     "I have something better than the board"
  2      standing 40, level 9+       the problem gets personal or bigger
  3      Trusted (60), level 12+     capstone: X earns the faction's item

* Offered by personal mail from the contact, in their established voice.
* Each mission is authored on top of an existing environment, with a twist,
  its own objectives and short nav-computer messages.
* Pay is above the board for the same risk. Failure gets the usual angry
  personal reply and the mission can be retried.
* Level gates spread the chains across levels 6-16, which is exactly the
  stretch where the game currently runs dry.

2. FACTION REWARDS
------------------
Standing should feel worth having at each step.

  STANDING         REWARD
  Favoured (25)    agent chain step 1 opens; market grade +1 (as now);
                   faction frame where it exists (as now)
  40               chain step 2; priority contracts: the board marks one or
                   two of that faction's jobs per refresh with +25% pay
  Trusted (60)     chain step 3 (the capstone); market grade +2 (as now)
  Capstone done    the faction's unique item; the contact's tone changes in
                   later mail; that faction's priority contracts pay +40%

Unique items: one per network, earned on the capstone mission, unsellable.
Each does something no market item does, without being a raw stat jump.

  ITEM               NETWORK      EFFECT
  Interdictor Array  OSD          Shooting down a projectile stalls the enemy
                                  that fired it for 1 s.
  Nomad Pod          Traders      +1 consumable slot on any frame.
  Ripper             Jackals      Unique weapon: heavy cannon that fires two
                                  rounds per shot (see section 3).
  Field Lattice      Helix        Shields keep rebuilding while you fire.
  Hot Box            QuickBite    Each new stage refills one charge of every
                                  fitted consumable.

* This also gives QuickBite standing its first real purpose.
* Following the style guide, an upgraded version of an earned item may
  appear in the market later, once normal progression catches up.
* Faction frames would also gain a level gate (Tier 4 frame at level 9 or
  higher, Tier 5 at level 14 or higher). Today a Tier 5 frame can open at
  level 3-4 through standing alone.


3. WEAPONS (KEPT, BUT SMALL)
----------------------------
Two new weapon families in the market alongside the Pulse Laser, plus the
Jackal unique. Same tiers, prices and colours as the laser.

  WEAPON            TIERS   GOOD AT                   WEAK AT
  Pulse Laser       I-V     everything (unchanged)    nothing in particular
  Scatter Emitter   II-V    swarms, shooting down     armour (0.6x damage)
                            incoming fire (wide hit)
  Rail Driver       II-V    armour and structures     slow, narrow, 2 shots
                            (3x damage, 2 vs ground)  per burst
  Ripper (unique)   -       Jackal capstone reward    -

* Each weapon gets a "structure damage" value for ground armour, walkers,
  generators, asteroids and doors. The laser keeps 1, so existing missions do
  not change. The Rail Driver at 2 is the first weapon that makes ground
  missions faster.
* Each needs its own gun model, shot streak and sound.
* Internals stay as they are for now, apart from adding the missing
  Targeting Computer I.


4. KEEPING THE BOARD ALIVE
--------------------------
Not new systems. Smaller jobs that make routine work last longer.

* Promote the DEV-only environments to normal board templates once their
  faction mission is built (dune armour, swamp delivery, swamp console,
  forest wreckage, space recovery).
* Let the four capped templates (station delivery, penthouse, terminal
  intrusion, forest cabin) scale to higher risk.
* Later, the random encounter idea from POSSIBLE GAME DIRECTION.txt: the
  contract says why X is there, and an encounter table decides what happens
  on the way. This is the long-term answer to repetition, and can come after
  the faction chains.


5. SUGGESTED BUILD ORDER
------------------------
  1. Pilot chain: one faction, three missions, mail and the unique item.
     Kudo is a good first choice: it reuses the most finished environments
     (station, dunes, bunker).
  2. Standing rewards: priority contracts and level gates on faction frames.
  3. The other four chains.
  4. Scatter Emitter and Rail Driver.
  5. Promote DEV environments to the board, and uncap the four templates.
  6. Random encounters.

Voice lines for new nav messages would be requested in the style guide's
copy-friendly format as each mission is built.
