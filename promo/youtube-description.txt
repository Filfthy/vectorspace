Synthwave Stampede

Herds of mech animals stampede across an alien world in one unbroken run, drawn in a retro synthwave outline vector style inspired by the classic vector games: the Star Wars arcade machine, Elite and Starglider.

Every frame was captured from the real-time engine of my upcoming game, Vector Space. No pre-rendered animation, no video effects added afterwards. What you see is the game drawing it.
