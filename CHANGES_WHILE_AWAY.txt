VECTOR SPACE: CHANGES MADE WHILE YOU WERE AWAY
==============================================

Started 27 Oct 2026, 21:11. Loop set to stop after 23:00.
Each entry is also its own git commit (git log shows the detail).
Everything was tested muted in the browser, but none of it hand-flown.

v520  Forest/swamp corridor now keeps its mission settings
      The corridor never stored the stage it was started with, so any mission
      that customised its ending (text, scan time, monolith layout) silently got
      the built-in defaults. Node 42's authored values happen to equal the
      defaults, so it looks the same, except its scan now ends with its own line
      "SCAN COMPLETE · RETURN DATA TO VOX".

      Performance check (no change needed). Frame cost in the heaviest scenes,
      1920x1080: elite fight with 20 fighters 3.1 ms average (worst 16 ms), dune
      field with 27 units 5.1 ms (worst 15 ms), xeno nest 2.1 ms. A 60 fps frame
      allows 16.7 ms. Elite fights only keep about 5 fighters active at once.

v530  Hostile standing now has a consequence
      While you are Hostile with a network (standing below -55), its enforcers
      become a likely route encounter on OTHER networks' jobs: OSD interdiction,
      Free Trader hired guns, Red Jackal hit squads, Helix security drones or
      QuickBite repo drones. Its own jobs never get them, so working for it is
      how you mend things. The Networks page and the Guide say so.
      Checked: Hostile networks still offer work, so standing can always be
      repaired.

v540  The debrief shows how standing moved
      After a job: "Standing · Helix Industrial Group  +8 · now 8 Neutral", plus a
      second line when a diversion moves another network (a rescued civilian
      gives OSD standing). Abandoning shows the loss in red.

      Save compatibility check (no change needed). A save made with the archived
      v292 build (level 9, AX-2R with Mk III laser, extender, consumables, early
      story progress) loads cleanly in the current build: equipment, charges,
      money and story flags intact, every hub panel opens without errors, and
      the new systems start working on it (faction agent offers arrive, 24
      achievements). Your own browser save should carry straight over.

v550  Kill callouts show the bounty
      Each kill now says what it earned as it happens: "HOSTILE DOWN · +CR 720",
      "WEDGE DOWN · +CR 550", "STRIDER BUCKLING · +CR 1,650". Armour kills had no
      callout before. Also fixed: since the v490 rebalance, heavy walkers were
      listed as "armour units" on the debrief.

v560  A fourth skill row for veterans (3 points each)
      All 12 skills used to be owned by about level 24, after which skill points
      meant nothing. New row: Deadeye (shots land 15% more easily on fighters),
      Hardened Field (+2 maximum shields), Heavy Warheads (missiles +40% damage)
      and Bounty Broker (bounties +25%). The full tree now takes until about
      level 36.

      Full regression sweep (no change needed). After tonight's changes I
      launched and stepped through every stage of all 12 story missions, the 15
      faction agent missions, the 6 Black Contracts and all 11 board templates:
      no errors anywhere. Station Delivery shows 9 of 10 stages because the truck
      stop arrival skips ahead when no fight is rolled, which is intended.

v570  Insurance screen names recovered faction hardware
      Faction reward items always came back when a drone was lost, but silently.
      The insurance screen now says "Faction hardware: Nomad Pod · Ripper ·
      RECOVERED FREE · back in stores", so a lost frame no longer looks like lost
      rewards.

v580  Contracts say what weapon grade they expect
      Enemy hulls get tougher in grade steps with risk, assuming your weapon
      keeps pace. Contract details now say "Expected weapon grade: Purple (IV)"
      and either "your Pulse Laser Mk IV is ready" or, in yellow, "your Pulse
      Laser is 3 grades below, so any fight will be long".

v590  Swapping a prepared contract says so
      Accepting a board contract while another one sits prepared in the drone
      bay used to swap them silently. Now a notice says "Now preparing Clear the
      freaking lane. Recovery Site Security is back on the board" (or "back in
      Mail" for story and vox contracts). Story mail buttons that were greyed
      out with no reason now read "FINISH CURRENT CONTRACT FIRST", like the vox
      ones already did.

v600  Voice lines for the new content: a list to record, and they just work
      Everything added since v400 (route encounters, enforcers, diversions) was
      text only. VOICE_REQUESTS.txt lists 16 short lines in the style guide's
      spoken-text-then-filename format. Save an mp3 into voices/ under the
      exact name and it plays: the game looks for each file at start-up, so no
      code change is needed. Until then nothing changes (on-screen text plus
      the older generic callouts). The pirate ambush now uses the existing
      "Pirate ambush! Destroy the pirates." recording. Side effect: the browser
      console shows a "404 file not found" for each line not yet recorded.
      That's harmless and goes away as you add them.

v640  Save to file / Load from file
      Browser saves belong to one address. Your campaign on the local server
      won't appear on GitHub Pages, and the reverse is true too. Settings >

v650  The hub no longer runs off small screens
      The terminal needs about 860px of layout width. On a 1024x640 window

      so the layout always fits: on a small window big text settings are held
      back, A+ greys out with "Larger text needs a bigger window", and the
      setting reads "130% · window limit". A window narrower than the layout
      (800x600) shrinks the hub to fit. Your setting is kept and applies fully
      on a bigger screen. Measured: 1024x640 and 800x600 at every setting,
      and 1920x1080, all with no overflow. Full-HD is unchanged apart from
      150% text, which is 2.23x instead of 2.25x.

v800  Fixes from a second independent review
      I had a fresh reviewer read everything from tonight. Nothing broke play,
      but it caught one thing that matters a lot:
      - say() (the old on-screen message) has deliberately done nothing for a
        long time. Your design keeps one text layer, the voice subtitles. So
        every on-screen prompt I added since v400 was invisible: route
        encounter announcements, "DISTRESS BEACON · INVESTIGATE OR CONTINUE",
        diversion outcomes, "HOSTILE DOWN · +CR 720" bounties and "LOYALTY
        CONFLICT". My tests checked the calls were made, not that you could
        see them. They now go through the subtitle line itself (callout()),
        but only when no spoken line is showing, so speech always wins and
        there is still only one text layer. The original game's own silenced
        messages stay silent. Checked on screen.
      - Groundside Pickup's depot handoff said "DATA UPLOAD" and "Data
        transfer complete" for taco shells. It now reads CARGO HANDOFF and
        CARGO RECEIVED with the "Delivery complete" voice. Wreck Recovery and
        Corvella's mission keep the data wording.
      - The world colour now also reaches the forest corridor floor and the
        city ground, instead of snapping back to green.
      - Hijacked-drone fights are acknowledged as drones in follow-up mail,
        not as pirates.

      - If you died or abandoned within a few seconds of an ace appearing,

v960  Fixes from a fourth independent review, and a title collection
      A fresh reviewer checked v920 to v950: nothing broke play, but it
      found five things, all fixed:
      - The epilogue missions skipped the risk toughness scaling, so their
        enemies were about 2.75x weaker than the capstone before them. Now
        scaled the same (Corvella's and Navira's epilogues were affected).
      - The debrief showed "standing +-3" for the smuggler cache's OSD row.
        It now reads "-3".
      - Titles overwrote each other, including Moonbreaker. Titles are now a
        collection: every one you earn is kept, the newest is worn, and the
        Operator page lists them as buttons so you can pick which to show.
        Your existing title is carried in.
      - The "missing smuggler cache" news headline ran after any three
        diversions. It now needs a cache you actually claimed (new stat).
      - The Networks line now says the final mission comes "a few jobs on",
        matching the five-job wait.

      Economy check (no change needed). With all the new board jobs in, I
      simulated three careers taking a random board job each time: 79 to 82
      jobs to reach level 20 (the earlier tuning aimed for about 81), and
      CR 1.76 to 1.81 million in contract pay, before bounties, favours,
      epilogues and Black Contracts. Milestones in one run: level 5 after 11
      jobs, level 10 after 34, level 15 after 58, level 20 after 82. Sixteen
      job types turned up in a single career. The new work didn't skew
      levelling or money.
