v520  Forest/swamp corridor now keeps its mission settings
      The corridor never stored the stage it was started with, so any mission
      that customised its ending (text, scan time, monolith layout) silently got
      the built-in defaults. Node 42's authored values happen to equal the
      defaults, so it looks the same, except its scan now ends with its own line
      "SCAN COMPLETE · RETURN DATA TO VOX".

      Performance check (no change needed). Frame cost in the heaviest scenes,
      1920x1080: elite fight with 20 fighters 3.1 ms average (worst 16 ms), dune
      field with 27 units 5.1 ms (worst 15 ms), xeno nest 2.1 ms. A 60 fps frame
      allows 16.7 ms. Elite fights only keep about 5 fighters active at once.

v530  Hostile standing now has a consequence
      While you are Hostile with a network (standing below -55), its enforcers
      become a likely route encounter on OTHER networks' jobs: OSD interdiction,
      Free Trader hired guns, Red Jackal hit squads, Helix security drones or
      QuickBite repo drones. Its own jobs never get them, so working for it is
      how you mend things. The Networks page and the Guide say so.
      Checked: Hostile networks still offer work, so standing can always be
      repaired.

v540  The debrief shows how standing moved
      After a job: "Standing · Helix Industrial Group  +8 · now 8 Neutral", plus a
      second line when a diversion moves another network (a rescued civilian
      gives OSD standing). Abandoning shows the loss in red.

      Save compatibility check (no change needed). A save made with the archived
      v292 build (level 9, AX-2R with Mk III laser, extender, consumables, early
      story progress) loads cleanly in the current build: equipment, charges,
      money and story flags intact, every hub panel opens without errors, and
      the new systems start working on it (faction agent offers arrive, 24
      achievements). Your own browser save should carry straight over.

v550  Kill callouts show the bounty
      Each kill now says what it earned as it happens: "HOSTILE DOWN · +CR 720",
      "WEDGE DOWN · +CR 550", "STRIDER BUCKLING · +CR 1,650". Armour kills had no
      callout before. Also fixed: since the v490 rebalance, heavy walkers were
      listed as "armour units" on the debrief.

v560  A fourth skill row for veterans (3 points each)
      All 12 skills used to be owned by about level 24, after which skill points
      meant nothing. New row: Deadeye (shots land 15% more easily on fighters),
      Hardened Field (+2 maximum shields), Heavy Warheads (missiles +40% damage)
      and Bounty Broker (bounties +25%). The full tree now takes until about
      level 36.

      Full regression sweep (no change needed). After tonight's changes I
      launched and stepped through every stage of all 12 story missions, the 15
      faction agent missions, the 6 Black Contracts and all 11 board templates:
      no errors anywhere. Station Delivery shows 9 of 10 stages because the truck
      stop arrival skips ahead when no fight is rolled, which is intended.

v570  Insurance screen names recovered faction hardware
      Faction reward items always came back when a drone was lost, but silently.
      The insurance screen now says "Faction hardware: Nomad Pod · Ripper ·
      RECOVERED FREE · back in stores", so a lost frame no longer looks like lost
      rewards.

v580  Contracts say what weapon grade they expect
      Enemy hulls get tougher in grade steps with risk, assuming your weapon
      keeps pace. Contract details now say "Expected weapon grade: Purple (IV)"
      and either "your Pulse Laser Mk IV is ready" or, in yellow, "your Pulse
      Laser is 3 grades below, so any fight will be long".

v590  Swapping a prepared contract says so
      Accepting a board contract while another one sits prepared in the drone
      bay used to swap them silently. Now a notice says "Now preparing Clear the

      Mail" for story and vox contracts). Story mail buttons that were greyed

  Hijacked military drones (orange, tougher, they stall in "loyalty

        every on-screen prompt I added since v400 was invisible: route

        CARGO RECEIVED with the "Delivery complete" voice. Wreck Recovery and

      - The world colour now also reaches the forest corridor floor and the
        city ground, instead of snapping back to green.
      - Hijacked-drone fights are acknowledged as drones in follow-up mail,
