Intonation tips. A full stop gives a falling, final tone. A comma gives a short pause with the voice held up. Two sentences ("Warning. Shields are low.") sound more urgent than one. A question mark lifts the end. Spell out awkward words the way they sound ("Vox" or "V O X"). Speed and pitch are small nudges: try ±5 to ±15 first.
